Mug Math

The 350 mugs at 24 mugs a box take 15 boxes, the last box holding 14 mugs.

Boundary: arithmetic only Mug Math counts an event mug stock with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the boxes the mugs take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a mug, and the minutes the printing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your mugs, your boxes or your press and does not know whether the mugs will really arrive unchipped, the press will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a box is worth its price or a mug is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 350 mugs, 24 mugs a box, 40 mugs a week, $38.50 a box and 6 mugs a minute.

The weeks truth

The 350 mugs at 40 mugs a week last 8 full weeks with 30 mugs left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 15 boxes at $38.50 a box come to $577.50, $1.65 a mug.

The time truth

The 350 mugs at 6 mugs a minute take 59 minutes, 0 hours 59 minutes.
