Mug Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event mug stock with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the boxes the mugs take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a mug, and the minutes the printing takes. It uses no named constants. The mugs, the box size, the weekly use, the box price and the printing speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your mugs, your boxes or your press and does not know whether the mugs will really arrive unchipped, the press will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a box is worth its price or a mug is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The boxes truth

Boxes: the mugs divided by the mugs a box, rounded up, with what the last box holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the mugs divided by the mugs used a week, with the mugs left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the boxes times the box price, to the cent, and spread over the mugs.

The time truth

Time: the mugs divided by the mugs printed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Mug Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
